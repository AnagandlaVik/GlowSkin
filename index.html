Tips for Your Goals
⏰ 5:30 AM Water Rule
Drink 34oz (1L) immediately on waking. This rehydrates your body after 7–8 hours without water, boosts metabolism by up to 30%, and helps your skin absorb morning skincare better.
💪 Pushups + Skin
Morning pushups increase blood circulation which delivers oxygen and nutrients to your skin cells — natural glow! Always cleanse your face after a workout to prevent sweat-triggered breakouts.
🧘 Stretch Benefits
Morning stretches reduce cortisol (stress hormone) — a top acne trigger. Even 5–10 min after waking sets a calm, focused tone that helps your skin stay clearer throughout the day.
🌿 Brightening Goal
Your Vitamin C serum (J Serum 12%) should always go on in the morning, on clean skin, before moisturizer. Always follow with SPF — UV exposure reverses every brightening product you use.
💧 Water + Glow
5L/day supports skin elasticity, reduces puffiness, and helps fade dark spots faster. Your Hyaluronic Acid serum works best when you're well hydrated from the inside out.
🧴 Acne Rule
Salicylic Acid cleanser = night only. Using it twice a day over-dries skin, triggering MORE oil = more acne. Never skip moisturizer, even with acne — dry skin produces excess oil to compensate.
🌸 Layering Order
Always layer thinnest → thickest: toner → serum → eye cream → moisturizer → SPF. Wait 30–60 seconds between each layer so each product fully absorbs before the next one goes on.
